# BRIEF — The Instrument (3dimensions.guide homepage)

Interviewed 2026-10-30. Tyler answered scope, grammar and the asset route in his own
words via a four-question pass; the signature move was explicitly delegated
("[No preference]") and is an **authored decision**, marked below.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Not re-asked. Settled by the live brand: dark premium, charcoal ground,
   parchment text, one accent per lens (amber Vehicle, emerald Road, crimson Stoplight),
   Playfair Display over DM Sans. References are the existing site and app, not new ones.
2. **The scroll journey.** Tyler's scope answer: *"Layer into the homepage"* — the new work
   replaces the framework section and the three-lens scroll story and sits above the
   calculator. Hero, calculator, pricing, FAQ, checkout are untouched.
3. **The energy curve.** Authored: quiet open, one loud middle, quiet hand-off. The page
   around it is already calm, so the instrument is the only place that raises its voice.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage, and the one moment.** See the feeling curve below. The one
   moment is the Stoplight act (authored, from the delegated signature move).
5. **One thing no other site does.** Delegated. Authored decision: *the instrument reads
   the real sky of the day you visit, and scrolling walks it through that day's hours.*
6. **How far from premium-minimal.** Not re-asked. The brand is premium-minimal and stays
   there; the instrument earns its difference from behaviour, not from loudness.
7. **One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?** Answered structurally: Tyler chose the
   **Live surface** grammar, which is neither. The page is one running surface.
8. **Assets.** Tyler asked the cost of generating through kie.ai rather than choosing.
   Answered: roughly $5 to $15 for this scope. Recommended against, because Live surface
   forbids the photography and the scrub device that generated footage exists to drive.
   **Route: no generation.** Real engine output, the existing seal, and art drawn in code.

## What this is and who it is for

A reflective self-navigation tool that reads one person's Human Design, Numerology and
Astrology from their exact birth moment. For someone who has met these three systems
separately and never seen them answer to each other.

## What the visitor must believe by the end

That this thing actually computes, and that it is reading the day rather than reading them,
only because they have not given it their birth details yet.

## What the visitor does next

One action, one label everywhere: **Calculate my profile**.

## The journey

```
1  Boot        a surface is already running, and it is reading today
2  Vehicle     today's machinery, named in plain words
3  Road        where today sits in the geometry
4  Sky         the real sky of this hour, moving under their hand   <- PEAK
5  The gap     three live readings, none of them theirs
```

## The feeling curve

```
1  Curiosity     a readout they did not ask for is already on screen and already moving
2  Recognition   the day's gate named plainly, and stated to be everyone's, not theirs
3  Orientation   one number, one line of fact, the quietest act on the page
4  Awe           the sky panel resolves into real positions and walks the day's hours   <- PEAK
5  Readiness     every panel lit, one field empty, the cursor already in it
```

No two adjacent feelings repeat. Orientation sits in front of Awe on purpose: awe needs
quiet in front of it.

## The peak

Act 4, Sky. The sentence a visitor would say to a friend:

> "The traffic light on it isn't a graphic. It's reading where the moon actually is right now,
> and when you scroll, you're moving through today."

It gets the largest span on the page by a visible margin, and act 3 is deliberately the
quietest thing in the section.

## The tell-someone sentence

It's the site where **the page is already reading the sky before you type anything, and the
only thing it is missing is you.**

## Authored silence

One. The end of act 3 holds with no movement for roughly a quarter of its span before act 4
begins. That is the silence in front of the peak, not dead scroll, and the verification pass
should expect it there and nowhere else.

## Hard rules inherited from this project

These outrank the skill where they disagree.

- No invented statistics, no fake dashboards. Every number on the surface is computed by our
  own engines from the real current date, or it does not appear.
- WCAG AAA contrast, 7:1 for body text. Colour is never the only signal: each lens carries a
  shape and a name as well.
- Reduce Motion gets the composition, not a stripped page.
- Nothing may slow the calculator down or move it.

## Grammar: Live surface, and why the other seven lost

Chosen by Tyler. The honest pitch for T3D is "watch what it does", and we genuinely compute,
so the demo is the argument.

- **Filmic one-shot** carries a burden of proof in the skill, and it forbids a second entry
  point. The homepage must let people jump to Pricing and the calculator, so it is out.
- **Chaptered editorial** forbids the fixed bar and puts no media above the fold. Our nav and
  hero are already built and earning.
- **Continuous world** requires worldflight and generated footage. Expensive, fragile, and
  the asset route is no-generation.
- **Typographic poster** bans cards and photographic ground, and cannot express computation,
  which is the one thing we have that nobody else does.
- **Gallery** needs a collection of objects. We have three lenses on one person, not a range.
- **Split stage** is an argument with exactly two sides. The triad is three.
- **Rhythmic cutlist** bans pinning and wants a pulse. Wrong feeling for an instrument.

**Scope deviation, stated plainly.** The skill assumes a grammar governs a whole standalone
page. Here it governs the instrument section, because Tyler chose to layer it into a working
commerce homepage. The site's existing top bar and hero stay. Inside the surface the
grammar's bans hold in full: no marketing chrome, no scrims, no full-bleed photography, no
display headings at hero scale, no scrub, no kinetic, no spotlight, drift no further than two
stops. The surface's own chrome is a real status strip that navigates.

## The signature move (authored under delegation)

**The instrument is on the day's clock.** Today's planetary positions are computed on the
server for each hour of the current date and shipped into the page as a small array. Scroll
position inside the Sky act maps to the hours of today: the Moon walks its real path, the
aspect readout recomputes, and the signal state changes when the real geometry changes. It
is written in the page off `--sc-p`, not as a parameter on an engine device, and no other
site can copy it without our ephemeris.

## Score table

| Act | Beat | Device | Why this one |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Boot | `flow` + `in`, staggered | The surface should look like it was already running before they arrived |
| 2 | Vehicle | `pin` + staged `cue` | The frame holds while the figure, then its words, then the drawing arrive in that order |
| 3 | Road | `reveal` (wipe) | A wipe is a change of state, which is what a new day number is. Quietest act, the silence before the peak |
| 4 | Sky | `pin`, long span, bespoke clock off `--sc-p`, `tilt` on the dial | The peak. The surface holds still while time moves, and the pointer answers, which is what operating something feels like |
| 5 | The gap | `flow` + `in`, no pin | The surface stops holding. Contrast with act 4 is the point, and the release hands straight to the real input |

Counters were planned for acts 2 and 3 and then cut in the build: the engine's counter
starts from zero, which puts a gate number of 0 and a day number of 0 on screen at the top
of the act. A figure that is briefly untrue is worse than a figure that simply arrives, and
the no-invented-numbers rule outranks the device. Five device families remain: flow/in, pin
with cues, reveal, pointer tilt, and the bespoke clock.

Checks: five distinct device families, no family twice in a row, zero `scrub` acts, one peak
with the largest span, no two adjacent feelings alike. Pinned spans are 1.4 and 2.6, and the measured section runs 5.4 viewport-heights on a
desktop and 5.8 on a phone, which stays clear of the 6-to-7 acts at 13.6-13.8vh band the
skill flags as a fingerprint.

## Verification (Step 5)

Walked in a headless Chrome at 1440x900 and 390x844, plus a reduced-motion pass.

- **Dead scroll:** none. The surface was sampled at sixteen positions across its whole
  length at both widths, counting elements actually painted on screen. The lowest count was
  seven; no step is blank. An earlier build failed this: the pinned acts gated their content
  on cues, and a pinned stage is on screen for a whole viewport before its pin begins, so
  the first screen of each act was empty. The panels now render in full and only the closing
  note arrives on a cue.
- **Cues that never reach full opacity:** none.
- **Contrast on the composited page:** every line measured against the real ground, with
  cues forced open. Zero failures against AAA (7:1 body, 4.5:1 large).
- **Reduce Motion:** zero elements left faded, the clock parks on the current hour, the dial
  stops tilting, and the composition is unchanged.
- **Tap targets:** no control under 44px at either width.
- **Overflow:** scrollWidth equals viewport width at 1440, 900, 640 and 390.
- **The close:** submitted a real birth date against the live preview endpoint. The surface
  switched from the day to the person, the three panels re-read, and the locked list
  appeared. No console errors.
- **Production build:** the homepage prerenders with the real reading in the HTML and an
  hourly revalidate.

**Not verified:** a real phone. Headless Chrome is not an iPhone, and the date field, the
sticky chrome and svh units are all worth a look on the actual device.

## Feel check

Scrolled cold, one word per act, before reopening this file: curiosity, recognition,
quiet, fascination, ready.

Against the intended curve, four of five match. Act 4 landed as fascination rather than awe.
The page was wrong, not the brief: the peak was emotionally the biggest moment but visually
no larger than the acts around it. The dial was enlarged to 64vh so the peak is also the
largest thing on the page, and the act's span was already the longest by a clear margin.
